What the rules do

Service date shift
Moves every DTP*472 date that sits inside a service line (loop 2400) — the ones opened by LX and closed by CLM, HL, or SE. Claim-level dates are left alone. Negative values shift backwards. Impossible dates are skipped rather than failing the file.
Find & replace
Rewrites element values only. Segment IDs are never touched, and neither is the fixed-width ISA header — changing a value's length there breaks the interchange for the receiver. Scope it to specific segments to keep a common string from being rewritten everywhere.

Two things it refuses to do

Replacement text containing this file's element separator or segment terminator is rejected instead of written, because it would split the segment it landed in. The component separator is allowed, since it is legal inside an element value.

Where your file goes

Nowhere. It is read in this tab, edited in memory, and handed back through your browser's download. Nothing is uploaded and nothing persists after you close the tab.
